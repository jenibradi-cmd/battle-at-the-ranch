# STILLWATER
### Brand 01 of the Gathering Place house · *the consecration of ordinary time*

> Stillwater sells **time, ritual, and belonging** — the felt experience of a slow life — and uses product as the membrane between the customer and that feeling. Every SKU answers one question: *"Does this make an ordinary moment feel consecrated?"* If it doesn't, it doesn't belong.

Stillwater is the anchor brand and the proof-of-concept for the Gathering Place [shared operating system](../../README.md). It owns **ritual** as its moat — the layer named competitors don't fully hold — especially **grief, Sabbath, and hospitality rituals**.

---

## Positioning

The intersection of **Toast's restraint**, **Flamingo Estate's ingredient theater**, **Blackberry Farm's estate-as-product**, **John Derian's curation-as-authorship**, and **RH's gravitational real estate** — with ritual as the unfair advantage.

**Customer:** the Gathering Place house customer — women ~38–62, $180k+ HHI, "time-affluent-seeking," flexes discernment over logos.

---

## Categories

Spa essentials · Ritual products · Home goods · Antiques · Garden · Jewelry · Gifts · Apparel & accessories · Experiences.

## Economics at a glance

- **Blended GM target:** ~58–62%.
- **Engine:** private-label spa/ritual consumables at 65–75% GM (replicable, refillable).
- **Credibility:** curated antiques (35–55% GM) + best-tier artisan goods.
- **Moat:** the owned signature fragrance accord + the ritual IP (especially the Grief Ritual).
- **LTV:** membership, subscription, and retreats convert customers into congregation.

## The first moves (from `STRATEGY.md`, Part 10)

1. **Lead with ritual** — build the Grief Ritual first (market-defining, near-unserved, press-generating).
2. **Fund with private-label consumables** — signature candle + bath salt + the refillable apothecary system.
3. **Borrow credibility** — best-tier artisan goods (Japan, Italy) + a curated antiques container.
4. **Own the scent** — one signature accord across candle/body/home (the recognition moat).
5. **Build congregation** — membership + ritual subscription + retreats.
